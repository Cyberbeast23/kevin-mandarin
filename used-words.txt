# Kevin Mandarin — words taught
# Day 1 (2026-10-04): greetings and polite basics
# Columns: Simplified Chinese, Hanyu Pinyin, English
# Tone notes (verified for this lesson):
#   你好 nǐ hǎo — both 3rd tone in citation form. In speech the first 3rd often rises (sounds like ní hǎo).
#   谢谢 xièxie — second syllable is neutral, not a full 4th tone.
#   不客气 bú kèqi — 不 is bù, but it changes to bú before the 4th tone of 客. 气 is neutral.
#   对不起 duìbuqǐ — the middle 不 is neutral (not bú / bù).
#   早上好 zǎoshang hǎo — in 早上 the 上 is usually neutral, not a full shàng.
#   什么 shénme and 名字 míngzi — 么 and 子 are neutral.

你好	nǐ hǎo	hello
再见	zài jiàn	goodbye
谢谢	xièxie	thank you
不客气	bú kèqi	you're welcome
对不起	duìbuqǐ	sorry
请	qǐng	please
早上好	zǎoshang hǎo	good morning
你叫什么名字	nǐ jiào shénme míngzi	what's your name

# Tone notes: doubled kinship words take a neutral second syllable (bàba, māma, gēge, jiějie, dìdi, mèimei); 子 in 儿子 is neutral. Introduced 两 liǎng before 个 in an example (not a headword).

# Day 4 (2026-10-07): food and drink
# Tone notes: 喝 hē stays level (1st). 很好吃 hěn hǎochī: first of two 3rd tones rises in speech. 一杯 yì bēi (一 before 1st tone). Example-only extras (not headwords): 饭 fàn, 吃饭 chī fàn, 喜欢 xǐhuan, 要 yào, 给 gěi, 杯 bēi, 碗 wǎn, 很 hěn, 这个 zhège, 我们 wǒmen, 吧 ba.
吃	chī	eat
喝	hē	drink
水	shuǐ	water
茶	chá	tea
咖啡	kāfēi	coffee
米饭	mǐfàn	(cooked) rice
面条	miàntiáo	noodles
好吃	hǎochī	delicious, tasty

# Day 5 (2026-10-08): time and days
# Tone notes: 几点 jǐ diǎn: first 3rd tone rises in speech (jí diǎn). 晚上 wǎnshang: 上 neutral. 一个星期 yí ge xīngqī (一 before underlying 4th-tone 个). Days of the week = 星期 + 一…六; Sunday 星期天/星期日. Example-only extras (not headwords): 星期四 xīngqīsì, 见 jiàn, 忙 máng, 有 yǒu, 吗 ma, 天 tiān, 点 diǎn, 八点 bā diǎn, 吃饭 chī fàn.
今天	jīntiān	today
明天	míngtiān	tomorrow
昨天	zuótiān	yesterday
现在	xiànzài	now
几点	jǐ diǎn	what time
星期	xīngqī	week
晚上	wǎnshang	evening, night
时间	shíjiān	time

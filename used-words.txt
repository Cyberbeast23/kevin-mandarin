# Tone notes (verified for this lesson):
#   你好 nǐ hǎo — both 3rd tone in citation form. In speech the first 3rd often rises (sounds like ní hǎo).
#   谢谢 xièxie — second syllable is neutral, not a full 4th tone.
#   不客气 bú kèqi — 不 is bù, but it changes to bú before the 4th tone of 客. 气 is neutral.
#   对不起 duìbuqǐ — the middle 不 is neutral (not bú / bù).
#   早上好 zǎoshang hǎo — in 早上 the 上 is usually neutral, not a full shàng.
#   什么 shénme and 名字 míngzi — 么 and 子 are neutral.

你好	nǐ hǎo	hello
再见	zài jiàn	goodbye
谢谢	xièxie	thank you
不客气	bú kèqi	you're welcome
对不起	duìbuqǐ	sorry
请	qǐng	please
早上好	zǎoshang hǎo	good morning
你叫什么名字	nǐ jiào shénme míngzi	what's your name

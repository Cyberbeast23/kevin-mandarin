🗣️ Mandarin Day 53: A Job Interview 面试

Kevin, Day 53! Today you're sitting across the table at a 面试 miànshì (job interview). Bring your 简历 jiǎnlì (résumé), take a breath so you're not 紧张 jǐnzhāng (nervous), and get 准备 zhǔnbèi (ready). The interviewer will ask for a 自我介绍 zìwǒ jièshào (introduce yourself), your 经验 jīngyàn (experience) and your 优点 yōudiǎn (strengths). Then the big question: the 工资 gōngzī (pay)! The line to keep is 我准备好了！ Wǒ zhǔnbèi hǎo le!, I'm ready! Old friends come back: 明天, 工作, 请, 把, 什么, 怎么样 and 慢慢. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Two falls: 面试 miànshì is 4-4: drop, then drop again.
  • Dip, then fall: 简历 jiǎnlì and 准备 zhǔnbèi are 3-4.
  • High, then something: 经验 jīngyàn is 1-4, 优点 yōudiǎn is 1-3, and 工资 gōngzī stays high: 1-1.
  • Dip, then high: 紧张 jǐnzhāng is 3-1. 自我介绍 zìwǒ jièshào is 4-3-4-4.
  • 一 changes tone: before 个 (4th tone), 一 becomes 2nd: 一个 yí ge. 别 + verb = don't: 别紧张 bié jǐnzhāng.

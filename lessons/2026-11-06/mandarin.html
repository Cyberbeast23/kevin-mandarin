🗣️ Mandarin Day 34: At the Gym 健身

Kevin, today we hit the gym: the fitness centre, working out, the treadmill, weightlifting, muscles, sweating, stretching before you start, and staying healthy. The phrase to keep is 锻炼对健康很好。 Duànliàn duì jiànkāng hěn hǎo., Exercise is very good for health! Old friends come back: 每天 and 去 from earlier days, 喜欢, 身体 from body/health, 跑步 from hobbies (now on a 跑步机), 先 from housework, and 很好. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Fall, high, rise: 健身房 jiànshēnfáng is 4-1-2 — sharp drop, flat high, then rise.
  • Two falling tones: 锻炼 duànliàn is 4-4. Keep both drops clear and even.
  • Dip, fall, high: 跑步机 pǎobùjī is 3-4-1. 举重 jǔzhòng is 3-4.
  • High, then fall: 肌肉 jīròu is 1-4. 出汗 chūhàn is also 1-4.
  • Two high flats: 拉伸 lāshēn is 1-1. Hold both syllables high and steady.
  • Fall, then high: 健康 jiànkāng is 4-1. Handy extras: 身体 shēntǐ, 结实 jiēshi (neutral second), and 以后 yǐhòu.

🗣️ Mandarin Day 51: At the Swimming Pool 在游泳池

Kevin, Day 51! Grab your swimsuit and towel, we're heading to the pool. You already know 游泳 yóuyǒng (to swim); add 池 chí (pool, pond) and you get 游泳池 yóuyǒngchí. Spot the water radical 氵 on the left of 游, 泳, 池, 深, 浅 and 潜: they are all about water! 水 shuǐ shows up twice: 跳水 tiàoshuǐ (jump + water) is to dive and 潜水 qiánshuǐ (hidden + water) is going underwater. 深 shēn and 浅 qiǎn are an opposite pair: deep and shallow. The line to keep is 别忘了带泳衣！ Bié wàng le dài yǒngyī!, Don't forget your swimsuit! Old friends come back: 游泳, 水, 吧, 我的, 很, 在, 大家, and 可以. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Three syllables: 游泳池 yóuyǒngchí is 2-3-2: rise, dip, rise. 救生员 jiùshēngyuán is 4-1-2: fall, high, rise.
  • Dip, then high: 泳衣 yǒngyī is 3-1. Rise, then high: 毛巾 máojīn is 2-1.
  • One-syllable opposites: 深 shēn (deep) is a high, flat 1st tone; 浅 qiǎn (shallow) dips with the 3rd tone.
  • Fall, then dip: 跳水 tiàoshuǐ is 4-3. Rise, then dip: 潜水 qiánshuǐ is 2-3.
  • Don't forget to …: 别忘了 bié wàng le + action: 别忘了带泳衣。 Whose: 是我的 shì wǒ de (is mine).

🗣️ Mandarin Day 49: Going to a Wedding 参加婚礼

Kevin, today you're a guest at a wedding: the bride and groom, the ring, a red envelope for the couple, a toast, and the best wish of all. Spot the pattern: 新 xīn (new) starts 新娘 xīnniáng (new + lady, the bride) and 新郎 xīnláng (new + young man, the groom), and the couple together are 新人 xīnrén, the new people. 婚 hūn (marriage) is in both 婚礼 hūnlǐ (marriage + ceremony) and 结婚 jiéhūn (tie + marriage, to get married). 干杯 gānbēi literally means dry the glass! The line to keep is 祝你们幸福！ Zhù nǐmen xìngfú!, Wishing you both happiness! Old friends come back: 朋友, 哥哥, 明年, 漂亮, 穿, 黑色, 给, and 戴上. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High, then rise: 新娘 xīnniáng and 新郎 xīnláng are both 1-2. Only the second syllable changes: niáng (bride) vs láng (groom).
  • Rise, then high: 结婚 jiéhūn and 红包 hóngbāo are 2-1. High, then dip: 婚礼 hūnlǐ is 1-3.
  • High and flat twice: 干杯 gānbēi is 1-1. Say it loud and bright!
  • Fall, then rise: 幸福 xìngfú is 4-2.
  • Light ending: 戒指 jièzhi is 4 + a short, light 指 zhi.
  • Wishing well: 祝 zhù + person + wish: 祝你们幸福！ Zhù nǐmen xìngfú! A toast to …: 为 wèi + person + 干杯！

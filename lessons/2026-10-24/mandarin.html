🗣️ Mandarin Day 21: Learning Chinese 学习中文

Kevin, it's Saturday, three weeks in, so today is about the thing you're actually doing: learning Chinese. These are the words you need in a real conversation when you're still a learner: study, Chinese, speak, listen, write, characters, understand, and question. The best phrase of the day is 我听不懂，请说慢一点 wǒ tīng bu dǒng, qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn — I don't understand, please speak a little slower. Old friends come back: 每天 and 喜欢 and 音乐 from hobbies, 请 from greetings, 可以 from making plans, and 很好 from day one. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Three single-syllable verbs: 说 shuō and 听 tīng are high and flat, 写 xiě dips. Say them as a set: shuō, tīng, xiě.
  • 听不懂: 不 goes light in the middle, so it's tīng bu dǒng, "listen, not, understand". Swap in 看 for 看不懂 (can't read it).
  • 中 and 汉 both point to China: 中文 Zhōngwén is the Chinese language, 汉字 Hànzì are the characters. Pinyin capitalizes them like English does.
  • One sandhi: 一 changes tone before a 4th tone, so 一遍 is yí biàn and 一个 is yí ge.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 再 zài again, 一遍 yí biàn one time, 字 zì a character, 怎么 zěnme how, 难 nán hard, 有意思 yǒu yìsi interesting, 慢一点 màn yìdiǎn a bit slower, 问 wèn to ask.

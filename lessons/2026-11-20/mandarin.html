🗣️ Mandarin Day 48: Working in the Garden 在花园里

Kevin, today you get your hands dirty in the garden: planting seeds, watering every day, watching them sprout and bloom, picking big red tomatoes, and chasing off the bugs. Spot the pattern: 花 huā (flower) shows up in 花园 huāyuán (flower + yard) and 开花 kāihuā (open + flower, to bloom). One character, two sounds: 种 is zhòng (4th tone) when it is the verb to plant, but zhǒng (3rd tone) in 种子 zhǒngzi, a seed. 浇水 jiāoshuǐ is pour + water. The line to keep is 种子发芽了！ Zhǒngzi fāyá le!, The seeds have sprouted! Old friends come back: 花, 叶子, 妈妈, 天气, 每天, and 又……又…… (both … and …). Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High, then rise: 花园 huāyuán is 1-2, and so is 发芽 fāyá.
  • High and flat twice: 开花 kāihuā is 1-1. High, then dip: 浇水 jiāoshuǐ is 1-3.
  • Same character, two tones: 种 zhòng (4, to plant) vs 种子 zhǒngzi (3 + light, seed). 我种的 is wǒ zhòng de.
  • Light endings: 种子 zhǒngzi and 虫子 chóngzi end in a short, light 子 zi.
  • Three syllables: 西红柿 xīhóngshì is 1-2-4 (literally western red persimmon).
  • It happened: 种子发芽了！ Zhǒngzi fāyá le! 了 shows the change has happened. Both … and …: 又大又红 yòu dà yòu hóng, big and red.

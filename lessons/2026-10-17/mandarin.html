🗣️ Mandarin Day 14: Hobbies and Free Time 爱好

Kevin, it's Saturday, so today is hobbies and the weekend: running, swimming, reading, music, and singing. The cover word is 爱好 àihào — hobby. Old friends come back: 喜欢 from animals, 晚上 from time, 家 from home, 早上 from greetings, and 很 everywhere. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 爱好 is a trick: 好 here is hào (4th, "to be fond of"), not the hǎo in 你好. So 爱好 is àihào, two falling tones.
  • 音乐 is another: 乐 reads yuè in music, but lè in 快乐 (happy). 音乐 = yīnyuè, high then falling.
  • 运动 and 跑步: 运动 yùndòng falls twice. 跑步 pǎobù dips then falls — the 3rd tone before a 4th is just a low half-dip.
  • 游泳: yóuyǒng rises then dips. 周末 zhōumò stays high then falls. 唱歌 chànggē and 看书 kàn shū both fall then go high.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 做 zuò to do, 每天 měitiān every day, 夏天 xiàtiān summer, 听 tīng to listen, 好听 hǎotīng nice to hear, 在 zài at.

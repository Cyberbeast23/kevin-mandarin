🗣️ Mandarin Day 17: Feelings and How You Are 高兴

Kevin, it's Tuesday, so today is all about feelings: happy, sad, tired, angry, worried, hungry, feeling well or not, and the everyday question 怎么样 zěnmeyàng — how are things? The cover word is 高兴 gāoxìng — happy. Old friends come back: 很 and 今天 from earlier days, 下班 from work, 吃饭 from food and drink, and 有点儿 from the body and health lesson. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Feelings take 很, not 是: say 我很累 wǒ hěn lèi (I'm tired), not 我是累. Here 很 is mostly glue, so it means "very" only lightly.
  • 了 for a change of state: 我饿了 wǒ è le means "I've got hungry (now)". 妈妈生气了 means Mom has become angry.
  • 饿 is just one vowel: è, a short falling "uh". 累 lèi also falls, like "lay!" said firmly.
  • 舒服 ends light: 服 is neutral, so shūfu is high then short. Put 不 in front for unwell: 不舒服 bù shūfu (不 stays 4th before a 1st tone). 怎么样 zěnmeyàng has a light 么 in the middle.
  • Steady pairs: 担心 dānxīn is two high flat tones. 生气 shēngqì is high then falling (气 is a full 4th here, not light like in 不客气). 难过 nánguò rises then falls. 高兴 gāoxìng is high then falling.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 认识 rènshi to know (a person), 他 tā he, 别 bié don't, 没问题 méi wèntí no problem, 吧 ba let's. 认识你很高兴 is the classic "nice to meet you".

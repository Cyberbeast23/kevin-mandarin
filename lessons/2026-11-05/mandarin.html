🗣️ Mandarin Day 33: At the Cinema 看电影

Kevin, today we head to the movies: film, the cinema, a good film to watch, finding your seat, when it starts and when it ends, the actors on screen, and a little popcorn. The phrase to keep is 这部电影很好看！ Zhè bù diànyǐng hěn hǎokàn!, This movie is really good! Old friends come back: 喜欢 and 看 from earlier days, 附近 from places, 七点 from time, 什么时候, and 一点 with tone sandhi. Measure word tip: films take 部 bù. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Fall, then dip: 电影 diànyǐng is 4-3, and 电影院 diànyǐngyuàn adds a falling 院.
  • Dip, then fall: 好看 hǎokàn is 3-4 — dip on 好, then a sharp drop on 看.
  • Two falling tones: 座位 zuòwèi is 4-4. Keep both drops clear.
  • High, then dip: 开始 kāishǐ is 1-3. 结束 jiéshù rises then falls (2-4).
  • Dip, then rise: 演员 yǎnyuán is 3-2. 爆米花 bàomǐhuā is 4-3-1.
  • Handy extras: 一点 sandhi yìdiǎn (not yīdiǎn), measure 部 bù for films (这部电影), and 有名 yǒumíng famous in the actor example.

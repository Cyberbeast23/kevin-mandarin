🗣️ Mandarin Day 37: A Snowy Winter Day 冬天的雪

Kevin, winter is on its way, so today we bundle up: winter itself, snowmen, skiing, gloves, a scarf, feeling warm and cosy, ice, and the puffer coat everyone in China calls 羽绒服. The phrase to keep is 外面很冷，别忘了戴手套。 Wàimiàn hěn lěng, bié wàng le dài shǒutào., It's cold outside, don't forget your gloves. Old friends come back: 冷 and 下雪 from the weather day, 周末, 妈妈, 今天, and 穿. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Two highs: 冬天 dōngtiān is 1-1 — keep it flat and high the whole way.
  • Dip, then rise: 雪人 xuěrén is 3-2. 雪 is xuě, the same 雪 as in 下雪.
  • Rise, then dip: 滑雪 huáxuě is 2-3.
  • Dip, then fall: 手套 shǒutào is 3-4.
  • Rise, then high: 围巾 wéijīn is 2-1.
  • Light ending: 暖和 nuǎnhuo is 3 plus a neutral 和 (huo, not hé). 很暖和 sounds like hén nuǎnhuo.
  • Short and high: 冰 bīng is 1st tone. 羽绒服 yǔróngfú is 3-2-2.
  • Handy extras: 堆雪人 duī xuěrén means build a snowman; 戴 dài is to wear gloves, scarves and hats; 条 tiáo is the measure word for a scarf.

🗣️ Mandarin Day 4: Food and Drink 吃和喝

Kevin, today is food and drink: the two verbs you need at every meal, water, tea and coffee, rice and noodles, and how to say the food is good. Eight words, each with a sentence you could use at a restaurant or the dinner table. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 吃 vs 喝: 吃 chī is for food you chew, 喝 hē is for drinks. Both are high, flat 1st tones. Keep 喝 hē level; it is easy to let it fall.
  • The "ch" and "sh" sounds: in 吃 chī, 茶 chá, and 水 shuǐ, curl the tip of your tongue back a little. The i in chī is not "ee"; it is a buzzy sound held right where the "ch" ends.
  • 好吃 and 很好吃: 好 hǎo and 很 hěn are both 3rd tone. When two 3rd tones meet, the first one rises, so 很好吃 sounds like hén hǎo chī. Pinyin still writes hěn hǎochī.
  • 一 again: one glass of water is 一杯水 yì bēi shuǐ. 杯 bēi is 1st tone, so 一 becomes yì, the Day 2 rule in action. And 两碗米饭 liǎng wǎn mǐfàn uses 两 from yesterday.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 喜欢 xǐhuan like, 要 yào want, 给我 gěi wǒ give me, 杯 bēi glass or cup, 碗 wǎn bowl, 这个 zhège this one. 饭 fàn on its own means a meal, so 吃饭 chī fàn is simply "to eat".

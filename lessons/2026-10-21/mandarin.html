🗣️ Mandarin Day 18: Making Plans with Friends 一起

Kevin, it's Wednesday, so today is about making plans: friends, doing things together, asking if someone is free, setting a time to meet, calling, waiting, saying a day works, and 一会儿见 yíhuìr jiàn — see you in a bit. The cover word is 一起 yìqǐ — together. Old friends come back: 吃饭 from food and drink, 明天, 几点, 晚上 and 星期 from time and days, and 请 from greetings. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 一 changes its tone: before a 1st, 2nd or 3rd tone it becomes 4th, so 一起 is yìqǐ. Before a 4th tone it becomes 2nd, so 一会儿 is yíhuìr and 一下 is yíxià.
  • Two 3rd tones in a row: the first one rises, so 可以 kěyǐ sounds like "ké-yǐ". 有空 yǒu kòng stays a low dip then a fall.
  • 朋友 ends light: 友 is neutral, so péngyou is rising then short.
  • 儿 at the end (erhua): 一会儿 is said as one curled-tongue syllable at the end, huìr, a Beijing-style touch. Some southern speakers just say 一会 yíhuì.
  • Two falling tones: 见面 jiànmiàn and 电话 diànhuà are both 4th then 4th; let the second one fall a little less.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 的 de (possessive, like 's), 给 gěi to or for, 打电话 dǎ diànhuà to make a phone call, 一下 yíxià a moment, 见 jiàn to see. 一会儿见 is a casual "see you soon".

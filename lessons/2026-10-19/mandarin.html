🗣️ Mandarin Day 16: Places and Directions 哪里

Kevin, it's Monday, so today is getting around town: asking where something is, left and right, going straight, and nearby places like the bank and the supermarket. The cover word is 哪里 nǎlǐ — where. Old friends come back: 在 from work, 去 and 坐 from travel, 买 and 商店 from shopping, 要 and 水 from food and drink. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 哪里 has a hidden tone change: it is written nǎlǐ, two 3rd tones. When two 3rd tones meet, the first one rises, so it sounds like nálǐ. In fast speech 里 often goes light too. 这里 zhèlǐ is a plain fall then dip.
  • 左边 and 右边 end light: 边 is neutral in speech, so zuǒbian dips then goes short, and yòubian falls then goes short.
  • 一 changes tone: 一 alone is yī, but before 直 (2nd tone) it becomes 4th: yìzhí zǒu.
  • 银行 uses háng: 行 is háng here (a trade, a firm), not xíng (to walk, OK). So 银行 is yínháng, two rising tones. 附近 fùjìn is two falls. 超市 chāoshì is high then falling.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 洗手间 xǐshǒujiān washroom, 在 zài is at, 有 yǒu there is, 吗 ma question marker, 就到了 jiù dào le and you're there. Pattern: thing + 在 + 哪里？ = Where is the thing?

🗣️ Mandarin Day 13: Clothes 衣服

Kevin, today is clothes: what you wear from head to toe, plus the verb 穿 and the handy word 新. The cover word is 衣服 yīfu — clothes. Old friends come back: 喜欢 and 漂亮 from recent days, 黑色 from colours, 冷 and 今天 from weather/time, and 买 from shopping. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 衣服 and the -zi words: 衣 yī stays high; 服 is usually neutral (yīfu). Same pattern for 裤子 kùzi, 鞋子 xiézi, 帽子 màozi — the 子 is light, not a full 3rd tone.
  • 衬衫 and 外套: 衬衫 chènshān falls then stays high. 外套 wàitào falls twice — keep both syllables sharp.
  • 穿 and 新: both 1st tone — 穿 chuān and 新 xīn stay high and flat. Easy to under-articulate; hold the pitch.
  • Measure words for clothes: 件 jiàn for shirts, jackets, and clothes in general (一件衣服). 条 tiáo for pants. 双 shuāng for a pair of shoes. 顶 dǐng for hats.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 件 jiàn, 条 tiáo, 戴 dài to wear (hats/glasses), 外面 wàimian outside, 了 le (completed action).

🗣️ Mandarin Day 28: At the Barbershop 在理发店

Kevin, today you're getting a haircut in China: the barbershop, getting a haircut, your hair, cutting it, short and long, the mirror, and a hair wash first. The phrase to keep is 请剪短一点 qǐng jiǎn duǎn yìdiǎn, please cut it a little shorter. Old friends come back: 附近 from directions, 想 from the post office, 可以 from making plans, and 看一下 from the hotel. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Dip, fall, fall: 理发店 lǐfàdiàn dips low, then drops twice. 理发 lǐfà is the first two of those.
  • Rise, then light: 头发 tóufa rises on 头, and 发 is short and light.
  • Two dips back to back: 剪短 is written jiǎn duǎn, but when two 3rd tones meet, the first one rises, so you say jián duǎn. 洗头 xǐtóu is a dip, then a rise, with no change.
  • Long rises: 长 cháng lifts up, like the hair is growing.
  • Fall, then light: 镜子 jìngzi drops on 镜, and 子 is light.
  • Sandhi with 一 and 不: 一点 is yìdiǎn and 一下 is yíxià. 不 turns 2nd tone before a 4th tone, so 不要 is bú yào.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 太 tài too, 一点 yìdiǎn a little, 后面 hòumian the back, 前面 qiánmian the front, 不要 bú yào don't, 先 xiān first, 了 le (change of state).

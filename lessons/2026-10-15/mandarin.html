🗣️ Mandarin Day 12: Animals 动物

Kevin, today is animals: dog, cat, bird, fish and horse, plus how to say you like something and call it cute. The cover word is 动物 dòngwù — animal. Old friends come back: 家 and 有 from home day, 吃 from food day, and 喜欢 (now a real headword after sitting in the examples for a while). Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 动物 falls twice: 动 dòng drops, 物 wù also drops — two 4th tones in a row. Don't flatten the second one.
  • Short animal names: 狗 gǒu and 马 mǎ both dip (3rd). 猫 māo stays high and flat. 鸟 niǎo dips. 鱼 yú rises.
  • 喜欢 in citation vs speech: written as xǐhuān (dip then high). In fast speech the second syllable often lightens to xǐhuan — both are fine.
  • 可爱 needs the apostrophe: 可 kě dips, 爱 ài falls. Write kě'ài so it isn't read as one run-on syllable.
  • Measure word 只: for most animals say 一只狗, 一只猫, 一只鸟 (yì zhī …). Horses take 匹 pǐ: 一匹马.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 只 zhī (measure for animals), 匹 pǐ (for horses), 树 shù tree, 小 xiǎo small/young, 大 dà big.

🗣️ Mandarin Day 1: Greetings and polite basics 问候

Kevin, this is lesson one. Eight everyday phrases: hello, goodbye, thanks, you're welcome, sorry, please, good morning, and asking someone's name. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 不 changes: bù is said bú right before a 4th tone, so 不客气 is bú kèqi. In 对不起 the middle syllable is neutral: duìbuqǐ.
  • Two 3rd tones: 你好 is written nǐ hǎo. In real speech the first one usually rises, so it can sound like ní hǎo. Type the written form.
  • Light syllables: 谢谢 xièxie, 早上 zǎoshang (上 is light, not a full shàng), 什么 shénme, 名字 míngzi.

🗣️ Mandarin Day 3: Family 家人

Kevin, today is family: parents, brothers and sisters, and your kids. Eight words, each with a short sentence you could really say about your own family, including one about Ace. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks. Notice how many family words just say one syllable twice.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Doubled words: in 爸爸 bàba, 妈妈 māma, 哥哥 gēge, 姐姐 jiějie, 弟弟 dìdi, and 妹妹 mèimei, the second syllable is light and quick (neutral tone). Put the weight on the first one.
  • Older vs younger: Mandarin always says which. 哥哥 older brother, 弟弟 younger brother, 姐姐 older sister, 妹妹 younger sister. There is no everyday word for just "brother".
  • The ü in 女儿: nǚ has the ü sound, like French "tu" (round your lips and say "ee"). Type it as v or u:, so nv3 er2 works.
  • 两 for two of something: as promised yesterday, two daughters is 两个女儿 liǎng ge nǚ'ér, not 二个. Use 二 èr for counting, 两 liǎng before a measure word like 个.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 我的 wǒ de means "my", but with family it is usually dropped (我爸爸 = my dad). 吗 ma at the end turns a sentence into a yes/no question. 岁 suì means years old.

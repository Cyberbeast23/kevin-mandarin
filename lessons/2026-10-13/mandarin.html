🗣️ Mandarin Day 10: Home and Daily Routine 家

Kevin, today is home and your daily routine: where you live, the rooms, the bed (Molly's favourite spot), and the times you get up, shower and go to sleep. Day 5's 几点, 早上 and 晚上 come back, and yesterday's 哪儿 too. Eight words, each with a sentence you could use about your own day. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 厨房 rises twice: 厨房 chúfáng is two 2nd tones, and 床 chuáng rises too. 房间 fángjiān rises, then stays high.
  • 睡觉 falls twice: 睡觉 shuìjiào is two 4th tones, short and firm, like saying "bed! now!"
  • Two 3rd tones: 洗澡 xǐzǎo sounds like xízǎo, the same rule as 你好. 起床 qǐchuáng dips, then rises.
  • 在 for where: 在 zài means at or in. 妈妈在厨房 is Mom is in the kitchen, and 住在哪儿？ is live where? Put the place after 在.
  • Time comes first: 我早上洗澡 is literally I morning shower. The time goes before the verb, as in Day 5: 我晚上十点睡觉.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 在 zài at/in, 欢迎 huānyíng welcome, 来 lái to come, 小 xiǎo small, 狗 gǒu dog, 床上 chuáng shang on the bed.

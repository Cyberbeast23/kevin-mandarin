🗣️ Mandarin Day 6: Weather 天气

Kevin, today is weather: hot and cold, rain and snow, sunny days, wind, and the umbrella every Vancouverite needs. Paired with yesterday's 今天, 明天 and 晚上, you can now chat about the forecast, which is the easiest small talk there is in any language. Eight words, each with a sentence you could say to a patient in the waiting room. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 气 is a full tone here: in 天气 tiānqì the 气 is a clear falling 4th tone. Compare Day 1's 不客气 bú kèqi, where the same 气 goes light and neutral.
  • Weather "falls": 下 xià means down, so 下雨 xià yǔ is literally "fall rain" and 下雪 xià xuě is "fall snow". Both are 4th then 3rd: drop, then dip.
  • Two 3rd tones in a row: 雨伞 yǔsǎn sounds like yúsǎn, and 很冷 hěn lěng sounds like hén lěng. Same rule as 你好. Pinyin still writes the 3rd-tone marks.
  • No "is" with adjectives: say 今天很热 (today very hot), not 今天是热. The 很 hěn is there for rhythm and doesn't always mean "very". 是 shì (is) only links two nouns: 今天是晴天, today is a sunny day.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 是 shì is, 了 le marks something that already happened (下雪了 it snowed), 大 dà big (风很大 is very windy), 没有 méiyǒu don't have (have is never negated with 不). Today is Friday: 今天星期五.

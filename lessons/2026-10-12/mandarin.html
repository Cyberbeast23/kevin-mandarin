🗣️ Mandarin Day 9: The Body and Health 身体

Kevin, today is the body and health, the words you'd hear first in a clinic: head, hands, feet, back, lower back, where it hurts, and the doctor. Day 6's 冷 and yesterday's 哪儿 come back, and you'll learn to ask a patient 哪儿疼？ Eight words, each with a sentence you could use with a patient or a friend. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 头 and 疼 both rise: 头 tóu and 疼 téng are 2nd tones, so 头疼 tóu téng (headache) climbs twice, like asking "what? what?"
  • The pain pattern: body part + 疼. 背疼 bèi téng back pain, 腰疼 yāo téng low back pain, 脚疼 jiǎo téng sore foot. Add 吗 ma to ask: 腰疼吗？ Does your lower back hurt?
  • 背 vs 腰: 背 bèi is the back in general, especially the upper and mid back. 腰 yāo is the waist and lower back, the usual word patients use for lumbar pain.
  • Two 3rd tones: 有点儿 yǒudiǎnr (a little) sounds like yóudiǎnr, and 你好 rules apply. 身体 shēntǐ is high and flat, then a dip.
  • 的 for my: 我的 wǒ de is my and 你的 nǐ de is your. With your own body you can drop it: 我头疼 is more natural than 我的头疼.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 的 de (possessive), 有点儿 yǒudiǎnr a little, 是 shì to be, 哪儿 nǎr where. 你身体好吗？ is a warm way to ask how someone's been keeping.

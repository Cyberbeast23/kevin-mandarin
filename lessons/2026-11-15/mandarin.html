🗣️ Mandarin Day 43: Renting an Apartment 租房子

Kevin, today you go apartment hunting: the apartment itself, the landlord, the rent, bedrooms, furniture, the deposit, the neighbours, and moving day. Spot the pattern: 房 fáng means house or room, and it builds a family: 房东 landlord (house-east), 房租 rent (house-rent), and old friends 房间 and 厨房. Apartments are counted with 套 tào (a set): 一套公寓. The question to keep is 房租每个月多少钱？ Fángzū měi ge yuè duōshao qián?, How much is the rent each month? Old friends come back: 租, 安静, 多少钱, and 下个月. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High, then fall: 公寓 gōngyù is 1-4, and so is 家具 jiājù.
  • Rise, then high: 房东 fángdōng, 房租 fángzū, and 邻居 línjū are all 2-1.
  • Fall, fall: 卧室 wòshì is 4-4. Drop the first one a little less so the second still falls.
  • High, high: 押金 yājīn and 搬家 bānjiā are 1-1. Keep both flat and high.
  • Measure word 套: 一套公寓 yí tào gōngyù (a set, a whole unit). 一 rises to yí before the 4th-tone 套.
  • Per month: 每个月 měi ge yuè. Next month is 下个月 xià ge yuè.
  • Neutral tones: 房子 fángzi and 房子里 fángzi li are light at the end.

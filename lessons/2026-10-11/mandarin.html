🗣️ Mandarin Day 8: Travel 出行

Kevin, today is travel and getting around: going places, taking the train, the subway, a taxi or a plane, and asking if somewhere is far. Yesterday's 商店, 贵 and 便宜 come along for the ride, and Day 5's 明天 lets you say when you're going. Eight words, each with a sentence you could use at a station or a hotel front desk. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 去 and 坐 both fall: 去 qù and 坐 zuò are sharp 4th tones. The pinyin q sounds like "ch" in cheese, so qù is roughly "chü", with rounded lips.
  • Word order for travel: who, then when, then how, then 去 + where. 我明天坐火车去机场 Wǒ míngtiān zuò huǒchē qù jīchǎng: I'm taking the train to the airport tomorrow. The vehicle comes before 去, never after the place.
  • All high and flat: 飞机 fēijī and 出租车 chūzūchē are 1st tone on every syllable. Keep your voice high and level, no dips.
  • Two 3rd tones: in 很远 hěn yuǎn (very far) the first 3rd tone rises, so it sounds like hén yuǎn. Same rule as 你好.
  • 车 means vehicle: 火车 huǒchē is literally fire vehicle (from steam trains), and 出租车 chūzūchē is rent-out vehicle.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 站 zhàn station, 吗 ma turns a sentence into a yes/no question, 大 dà big, 近 jìn near. Today is Sunday: 今天星期天 jīntiān xīngqītiān.

🗣️ Mandarin Day 38: At the Dentist 看牙医

Kevin, today we sit in the dentist's chair: the dentist, teeth, brushing, a toothbrush, toothpaste, opening wide, booking an appointment, and getting a check-up. Spot the pattern: 牙 yá (tooth) shows up in almost every word. The phrase to keep is 请张嘴，我看一下。 Qǐng zhāng zuǐ, wǒ kàn yíxià., Please open your mouth, let me take a look. Handy for any clinic. Old friends come back: 医生, 明天, 每天, 星期二, and 一下. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • The tooth syllable: 牙 yá is always 2nd tone, rising. 牙医 yáyī is 2-1 and 牙膏 yágāo is 2-1 too.
  • Swap the order: 刷牙 shuāyá (1-2) is the action, brush teeth. 牙刷 yáshuā (2-1) is the thing, a toothbrush. Same two characters, flipped.
  • Rise, then dip: 牙齿 yáchǐ is 2-3. 齿 is chǐ, with a curled-tongue ch.
  • High, then dip: 张嘴 zhāng zuǐ is 1-3. 嘴 is the mouth.
  • Fall, then high: 预约 yùyuē is 4-1. The ü sound is in both syllables: round your lips.
  • Dip, then rise: 检查 jiǎnchá is 3-2.
  • Handy extras: 看牙医 kàn yáyī means go to the dentist; 刷两次牙 shuā liǎng cì yá puts the count in the middle; 用完了 yòng wán le means used up.

🗣️ Mandarin Day 23: Halloween Is Coming 万圣节快到了

Kevin, Halloween is this Saturday, so today's words are all about it: Halloween, holiday, pumpkin, candy, ghost, being scared, dressing up, and masks. The phrase to keep is 你今年打扮成什么？ nǐ jīnnián dǎban chéng shénme, what are you dressing up as this year? Old friends come back: 喜欢 from hobbies, 吃 from food, 买 from shopping, and 爸爸 from family. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Falling, falling, rising: 万圣节 Wànshèngjié drops twice, then lifts at the end. It is a name, so pinyin gives it a capital.
  • Two falls: 害怕 hàipà and 面具 miànjù both drop sharply twice.
  • Full dip alone: 鬼 guǐ said by itself gets the whole low dip and rise back up.
  • Light 扮: 打扮 is dǎban, with 扮 short and neutral in everyday speech.
  • One sandhi: 一 changes to 2nd tone before a 4th tone, so 一个 is yí ge.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 快…了 kuài … le almost, 最 zuì most, 孩子们 háizimen kids, 相信 xiāngxìn to believe, 别 bié don't, 成 chéng into / as, 可怕 kěpà scary, 南瓜灯 nánguā dēng jack-o'-lantern.

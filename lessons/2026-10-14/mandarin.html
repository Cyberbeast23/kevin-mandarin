🗣️ Mandarin Day 11: Colors and Describing Things 颜色

Kevin, today is colors: red, blue, green, yellow, white and black, plus how to ask someone's favourite colour and say something looks pretty. Almost every colour word ends in 色 sè, so learn that one syllable and you're halfway there. Old friends come back in the examples: the umbrella from Day 6, the taxi from Day 8, and 要 and 多少钱 from shopping day. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 色 always falls: 色 sè is a sharp 4th tone. So most colours are "first syllable + a drop": 红色 hóngsè, 蓝色 lánsè, 黄色 huángsè, 白色 báisè all rise, then fall.
  • The odd ones out: 黑色 hēisè starts high and flat, and 绿色 lǜsè falls twice.
  • The ü in 绿: 绿 lǜ keeps its two dots after l, because lù (road) is a different word. Round your lips as if saying "oo", then say "ee". You can type it as lv4 or lu:4.
  • 漂亮 ends light: 漂亮 piàoliang falls, then the 亮 is short and neutral.
  • 是…的 for describing: 我的车是红色的 is literally my car is red-one. Drop the noun and 白色的 alone means the white one.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 喜欢 xǐhuan to like, 车 chē car, 的 de the …one, 这个 zhège this one.

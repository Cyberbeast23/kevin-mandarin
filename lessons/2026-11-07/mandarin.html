🗣️ Mandarin Day 35: At the Library 图书馆

Kevin, today we visit the library: the library itself, books, borrowing and returning, staying quiet, reading, magazines, and the bookshelf. The phrase to keep is 我想借这本书。 Wǒ xiǎng jiè zhè běn shū., I want to borrow this book! Old friends come back: 想, 这, 去, 很, 喜欢, 看 from earlier days, plus the measure 本 for books. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Rise, high, dip: 图书馆 túshūguǎn is 2-1-3 — rise, flat high, then dip.
  • One high flat: 书 shū is 1st tone. Hold it high and steady.
  • Sharp fall: 借 jiè is 4th tone — borrow. Drop quickly.
  • Rising (not falling!): 还 huán meaning “to return (a book)” is 2nd tone (not hái “still/also”). Rise up.
  • High, then fall: 安静 ānjìng is 1-4. 书架 shūjià is also 1-4.
  • Fall, then rise: 阅读 yuèdú is 4-2. 杂志 zázhì is 2-4.
  • Handy extras: measure 本 běn for books; 喜欢 xǐhuan (neutral second); 明天 míngtiān.

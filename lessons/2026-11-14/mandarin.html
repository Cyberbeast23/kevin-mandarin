🗣️ Mandarin Day 42: Watching a Hockey Game 看冰球比赛

Kevin, it's Saturday night, so today we watch hockey: the sport itself, the game, the team, the fans, scoring a goal, winning, losing, and cheering your team on. Spot the pattern: 球 qiú means ball (or puck), and it builds a family: 冰球 ice hockey, 球队 team, 球迷 fan, 进球 goal. The phrase to keep is 加油！ Jiāyóu!, Go! Come on! It's the same 加油 as in 加油站 (gas station): literally add fuel. Old friends come back: 看, 喜欢, 开始, 一起, and 太好了. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High, then rise: 冰球 bīngqiú is 1-2. Literally ice-ball. 加油 jiāyóu is also 1-2.
  • Dip, then fall: 比赛 bǐsài is 3-4. The 3rd tone before a 4th is just a short low note.
  • Rise, then fall: 球队 qiúduì is 2-4. Rise, rise: 球迷 qiúmí is 2-2 (迷 means crazy about).
  • Fall, then rise: 进球 jìnqiú is 4-2. Literally enter-ball.
  • Win and lose: 赢 yíng rises, 输 shū stays high and flat. Add 了 for a finished result: 赢了, 输了.
  • Scores use 比: 三比二 sān bǐ èr is three to two.
  • Watch 一: 一起 is yìqǐ (一 falls before a 3rd tone), and 一定 is yídìng (一 rises before a 4th tone).

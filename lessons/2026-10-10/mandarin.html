🗣️ Mandarin Day 7: Shopping 买东西

Kevin, today is shopping: buying, money, asking the price, and deciding if it's a bargain or a rip-off. Your Day 2 numbers come back to life here, because 五块 is five yuan and 十块 is ten. Put it together with Day 4's 茶 and 咖啡 and you can order a drink and pay for it. Eight words, each with a sentence you could use at a shop counter or a café. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • 买 vs 卖: 买 mǎi (dipping 3rd) is buy. Its twin 卖 mài (falling 4th) is sell, with an extra stroke on top. Only the tone tells them apart out loud, so dip clearly on 买.
  • 便宜 is not "biàn": 便 is usually biàn (convenient), but in 便宜 it reads pián, and 宜 goes light: piányi.
  • 多少 goes light: in everyday 多少钱 the 少 is soft, duōshao qián. You'll also hear a full duōshǎo; both are fine.
  • 块 = yuan, casually: prices are written 元 yuán but spoken as 块 kuài, like saying "bucks". Number first: 五块 wǔ kuài, five yuan.
  • 一 changes again: before 杯 bēi (a 1st tone) 一 becomes yì, so 一杯茶 is yì bēi chá, one cup of tea. Same rule as Day 2.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 这个 zhège this one, 太…了 tài … le too (much), 在 zài is at, 哪儿 nǎr where, 杯 bēi cup. Today is Saturday: 今天星期六.

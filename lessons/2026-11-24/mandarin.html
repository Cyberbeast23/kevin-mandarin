🗣️ Mandarin Day 52: Making Dumplings 包饺子

Kevin, Day 52! Roll up your sleeves, we're making dumplings. In Chinese you don't "make" dumplings, you wrap them: 包饺子 bāo jiǎozi. Start with 面粉 miànfěn (flour), 擀 gǎn (roll out) thin 皮 pí (wrappers), spoon in the 馅 xiàn (filling), then cook them: 煮 zhǔ (boil) or 蒸 zhēng (steam). Spot the fire radical 灬 at the bottom of 煮 and 蒸: both are cooking with heat! Finish with a dip in 醋 cù (vinegar). The line to keep is 我们一起包饺子吧！ Wǒmen yìqǐ bāo jiǎozi ba!, Let's make dumplings together! Old friends come back: 一起, 吧, 喜欢, 妈妈, 水, 太…了, and 的时候. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Dip, then light: 饺子 jiǎozi is 3 + neutral: the 子 is short and soft.
  • Fall, then dip: 面粉 miànfěn is 4-3.
  • One-syllable cooking verbs: 擀 gǎn and 煮 zhǔ dip (3rd); 蒸 zhēng is high and flat (1st).
  • Short and sharp: 馅 xiàn and 醋 cù fall (4th); 皮 pí rises (2nd).
  • Wrap, not make: 包饺子 bāo jiǎozi. 把 pattern: 把饺子放进去 bǎ jiǎozi fàng jìnqu (put the dumplings in).

🗣️ Mandarin Day 50: Taking the High-Speed Train 坐高铁

Kevin, Day 50! Today you're riding China's famous high-speed train: find your platform, your car, a window seat, and roll out right on time. Spot the pattern: 站 zhàn means station or stop, so 站台 zhàntái (station + stage) is the platform and 到站 dàozhàn (arrive + station) means you've reached your stop. 车 chē (vehicle) is back in 车厢 chēxiāng (vehicle + box, a train car). 高铁 gāotiě is short for high + iron (rail), and 铁 is the same tiě as in 地铁 and 拿铁! The line to keep is 到站了，我们下车吧！ Dàozhàn le, wǒmen xià chē ba!, We're at our stop, let's get off! Old friends come back: 坐, 北京, 号, 上车, 下车, 座位, 火车, and 点. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High and flat twice: 车厢 chēxiāng and 出发 chūfā are 1-1. Keep your voice up the whole way.
  • High, then dip: 高铁 gāotiě is 1-3. Dip, then rise: 准时 zhǔnshí is 3-2.
  • Fall, then rise: 站台 zhàntái is 4-2. Rise, then fall: 乘客 chéngkè is 2-4.
  • Fall, then high: 靠窗 kàochuāng is 4-1. Fall twice: 到站 dàozhàn is 4-4, like two firm taps.
  • Where it is: number + 号 hào + place: 三号站台 (platform 3), 五号车厢 (car 5). Let's …: end with 吧 ba: 我们下车吧！

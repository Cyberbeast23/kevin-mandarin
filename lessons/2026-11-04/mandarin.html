🗣️ Mandarin Day 32: Housework and Chores 做家务

Kevin, today is chore day: cleaning up, doing the laundry, the garbage and throwing it out, clean versus dirty, mopping the floor, and asking someone to lend a hand. The phrase to keep is 你可以帮忙吗？ Nǐ kěyǐ bāngmáng ma?, Can you give me a hand? Old friends come back: 房间 and 厨房 from home, 周末 from hobbies, 衣服 and 鞋子 from clothes, 碗 from the restaurant, and 可以 from making plans. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Third-tone sandhi: 打扫 dǎsǎo has two 3rd tones, so the first rises: it sounds like dá sǎo. 洗碗 xǐ wǎn and 可以 kěyǐ work the same way.
  • Light ending: 洗衣服 xǐ yīfu ends on a short, neutral 服.
  • High and flat: 垃圾 lājī is two high 1st tones, and so is 脏 zāng with 扔 rēng. Keep them level.
  • High, then drop: 干净 gānjìng and 拖地 tuōdì both stay high, then fall sharply.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 把 bǎ moves the object before the verb (把垃圾扔了 throw the garbage out), 先 xiān first, 洗碗 xǐ wǎn wash the dishes, 太…了 tài…le too, way too.

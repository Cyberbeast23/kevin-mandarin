🗣️ Mandarin Day 47: A Day at the Beach 去海边

Kevin, today you spend a sunny day by the sea: the sandy beach, the big waves, hunting for seashells, sunscreen and sunglasses, trying to surf, and going home with a tan. Spot the pattern: 海 hǎi (sea) shows up in 海边 hǎibiān (sea + side) and 海浪 hǎilàng (sea + wave), and 浪 làng comes back in 冲浪 chōnglàng, literally to charge the waves. 晒 shài means to sit in the sun: 防晒霜 fángshàishuāng is guard-sun-cream, and 晒黑 shàihēi is sun-black, a tan. The line to keep is 别忘了涂防晒霜。 Bié wàng le tú fángshàishuāng., Don't forget to put on sunscreen. Old friends come back: 常常, 儿子, 今天, 吧, and 会 (can, know how to). Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Low, then high: 海边 hǎibiān is 3-1. Low, then fall: 海浪 hǎilàng is 3-4.
  • High and flat twice: 沙滩 shātān is 1-1.
  • Fall, then rise: 贝壳 bèiké is 4-2.
  • Three syllables: 防晒霜 fángshàishuāng is 2-4-1, and 太阳镜 tàiyángjìng is 4-2-4 (太阳 is the sun, 镜 is glass or lens).
  • High, then fall: 冲浪 chōnglàng is 1-4. Fall, then high: 晒黑 shàihēi is 4-1.
  • Don't forget: 别忘了 + verb. 别忘了涂防晒霜。 Bié wàng le tú fángshàishuāng. 涂 tú is to spread or put on.
  • Got a tan: 我晒黑了。 Wǒ shàihēi le. 了 shows the change has happened.

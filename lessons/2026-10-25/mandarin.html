🗣️ Mandarin Day 22: Autumn in the Park 秋天的公园

Kevin, it's a late-October Sunday, the perfect day for a walk, so today's words are all about getting outside in the fall: autumn, park, going for a walk, trees, leaves, flowers, mountains, and fresh air. The phrase to keep is 周末我们去公园吧 zhōumò wǒmen qù gōngyuán ba, let's go to the park on the weekend. Old friends come back: 天气 and 舒服 from weather and feelings, 红色 and 黄色 from colours, 周末 and 喜欢 from hobbies, and 一起 from making plans. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High and flat all day: 秋天 qiūtiān, 花 huā, and 山 shān are all 1st tone. Keep your voice up and steady.
  • Two falls in a row: 散步 sànbù drops twice, like two firm steps.
  • Light 子: 叶子 is yèzi, with 子 short and neutral, just like 筷子 and 鞋子 from earlier days.
  • One sandhi: 一 changes tone before a 3rd tone, so 一起 is yìqǐ.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 吧 ba let's, 里 lǐ inside, 有 yǒu there is, 很多 hěn duō lots of, 和 hé and, 这些 zhèxiē these, 爬山 pá shān to hike (climb a mountain).

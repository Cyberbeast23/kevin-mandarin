🗣️ Mandarin Day 27: At the Post Office 在邮局

Kevin, today you're sending things in China: the post office, mailing something, a parcel, a letter, the address, a stamp, courier delivery, and getting it on the other end. The phrase to keep is 我想寄东西 wǒ xiǎng jì dōngxi, I'd like to send something. Old friends come back: 银行 from directions, 多少钱 from shopping, 朋友 from making plans, and 写 from learning Chinese. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • Two rises: 邮局 yóujú lifts twice, like asking a question twice.
  • Rise, then fall: 邮票 yóupiào goes up, then drops, like pressing a stamp down.
  • High, then dip: 包裹 bāoguǒ starts high and flat, then dips low.
  • Two falls: 快递 kuàidì drops sharply twice, quick like a courier.
  • Single falls: 寄 jì and 信 xìn are short and firm. 地址 dìzhǐ falls, then dips.
  • High, then fall: 收到 shōudào holds high, then drops.
  • One sandhi: 一 is 4th tone before 1st, 2nd and 3rd tones, so 一封 is yì fēng and 一张 is yì zhāng. Before a 4th tone it turns 2nd, as in 一下 yíxià.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 旁边 pángbiān next to, 想 xiǎng would like to, 东西 dōngxi things, 多重 duō zhòng how heavy, 封 fēng (counter for letters), 张 zhāng (counter for flat things), 到 dào to arrive, 吗 ma (question ending).

🗣️ Mandarin Day 25: Under the Weather 感冒了

Kevin, cold-and-flu season is here, so today is about feeling sick and getting better: getting sick, a cold, a fever, a cough, medicine, the pharmacy, resting, and drinking plenty of water. The phrase to keep is 我感冒了 wǒ gǎnmào le, I've caught a cold. Old friends come back: 舒服 from feelings, 附近 from directions, 喝水 from food and drink, and 妈妈 from family. Characters are Simplified Chinese. The pronunciation line is Hanyu Pinyin with tone marks.

How to read the tones:
  • 1st ā high and flat · 2nd á rising · 3rd ǎ dipping · 4th à falling. No mark means a short, light (neutral) syllable.
  • High and flat twice: 发烧 fāshāo stays up high the whole way, like a steady temperature reading.
  • Neutral second syllables: 咳嗽 késou and 休息 xiūxi end with a short, light tap.
  • Dip, then fall: 感冒 gǎnmào starts low and then drops hard.
  • Two falls: 药店 yàodiàn drops sharply twice. 药 alone is yào, the same sound as 要 (to want).
  • Flat, flat, dip: 多喝水 duō hē shuǐ holds high for two beats, then dips.
  • One sandhi: 一 changes to 4th tone before a 1st tone, so 一天 is yì tiān, one day.
  • Handy extras in the examples: 了 le marks a change (now sick), 吗 ma turns a sentence into a question, 次 cì times, 两次 liǎng cì twice, 好好 hǎohǎo properly, well, 在家 zài jiā at home, 早日康复 zǎorì kāngfù get well soon.
